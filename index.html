Video Games can be such an incredible art form and story telling medium. It’s incredible to see how pixels on a screen can really induce a rainbow of feelings on a human being, and to me there is no bigger satisfaction than creating content and experiences that induce these amazing emotions through the medium. That is why Game Design is my passion, my goal will always be creating these feelings of amusement, wonder, and joy hopefully inspiring others to be their best version.

Growing up in Venezuela I realized really early in my life how incredible video games can be, how they can be way more than just a distraction, a literal escape from reality to many. I started picking up games that allowed user generated content from an early age like Minecraft, Halo 4’s Forge Mode, GTA Online’s Creator in hopes to replicate these great feelings that all of my favorite games would make me feel. Many years later here I am actually designing video games for a living! A real dream come true, my 13-year-old self, who had just figured out he wanted to make games for a living, would’ve never imagined it.

Hiker and Kayaker. I love playing Tennis, I enjoy watching anime, huge Star Wars fan. Soccer fan (Hala Madrid!) and baseball fan (Go Padres!)

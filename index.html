To me there is no bigger satisfaction than seeing someone having fun with a mechanic, a mission, or a system you crafted from zero. That's why Game Design is my passion. Since the beginning of my Game Design career, my goal has been and will always be creating these feelings of amusement, serenity, and joy in players and inspire them to be their best version.

I grew up building "Mini-games" on Minecraft, I would create a creative world, make some rules, and write them on Minecraft signs and play them with friends. After that, I started designing levels on Halo 4's Forge Mode, and then I moved into Unity

I love playing Tennis, I enjoy watching anime, huge Star Wars fan. Love soccer and baseball (Go Padres!)
